[This guy](http://members.shaw.ca/infonic/icece/) use [Sun E450](http://sunsolve.sun.com/handbook_pub/Systems/E450/E450.html), running [Solaris 10](http://wwws.sun.com/software/solaris/10/), as his in-car mp3 player.

Multi-processor, for great audio quality.  
Loads of disks, serving different songs for a whole month.

Just an idea for your car audio solution 😉
