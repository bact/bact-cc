After a VERY boring 80 mins passed, Portugal’s Postiga started the best heat of the tournament.

A “[silver goal](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_goal)” rule gave the great excitement of the game, and its 2-2 when extra-time ends. One punch for one punch.

At the end of the day, Englishmen can (somewhat) proud of their team, shin up, while crying for their penalty lost. — It’s just not their day. Eriksson’s face can tells you everything.

**Quality game.**
