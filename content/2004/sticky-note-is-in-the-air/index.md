What will happen? How our live will changes? if the net is everywhere.

BBC News [reports](http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/technology/3860481.stm) on [Urban Tapesteries](http://urbantapestries.net/) project.  
A technology that allow you to leave note at a place you currently are to other people who will coming at that place in the future. It just like putting sticky note in the air.

**update:** Win EE6 just had [introduced](http://siit.net/webboard/read.php?Topic_ID=21360) me a quite similar project, [Geo Notes](http://2g1319.ssvl.kth.se/~csd2002-geonotes/) from Sweden.
