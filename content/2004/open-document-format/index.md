ของเก่า ขอเล่าใหม่อีกรอบ (ในบล็อก)

**[1dok.org](http://www.1dok.org/)** is an initiative by [European Union](http://www.eu.int/) to develop and standardize the document formats. It is based on the [OASIS Open Office XML Format](http://www.oasis-open.org/committees/tc_home.php?wg_abbrev=office), which itself based on the [OpenOffice.org XML File Format](http://xml.openoffice.org/).

June 03, 2004, Pavel Janik, an OpenOffice.org developer, blogged:  
[EU eGovernment policy-makers encourage the uptake of open document formats](http://blog.janik.cz/archives/permalinks/2004-06-03T09_07_42.html)
