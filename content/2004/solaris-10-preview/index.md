(first of all, it’s not a [free software](http://www.gnu.org/).  
but it has its place, like many many other things around you — right now.)

some of the interesting new/improved features:

– Chip Multithreading (improve performance)  
– [N1 Grid Containers](http://www.sun.com/2004-0330/feature/index.html) (improve utilization)  
– Predictive self healing (reduce downtime)  
– Dynamic File System (reduce admin works)  
– [DTrace](http://www.sun.com/2004-0518/feature/index.html) (real-time system tracking)  
– Cryptographic infrastructure (security)  
– Process rights management (security)  
– [Zones](http://www.sun.com/bigadmin/features/articles/solaris_zones.html) (security, resource management)

[full details @ sun.com](http://wwws.sun.com/software/solaris/10/)
