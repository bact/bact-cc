[OpenOffice.org 2.0](http://development.openoffice.org/releases/OpenOffice_org_trunk.html) will use [W3C XForms](http://www.w3.org/MarkUp/Forms/) standard for its form functionalities.

Microsoft Office 2003 also have the similar form functionalities, in its [InfoPath](http://www.microsoft.com/office/infopath/prodinfo/default.mspx). InfoPath is built on top of XPath and XSLT (among other technologies), but it doesn’t supports XForms, yet.

Mozilla will [supports](http://www.mozilla.org/projects/xforms/) XForms as well.
