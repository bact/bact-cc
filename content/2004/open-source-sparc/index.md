From its website:

*[LEON2](http://www.gaisler.com/products/leon2/leon.html) is a synthesisable VHDL model of a 32-bit processor compliant with the [SPARC V8 architecture](http://www.sparc.com/). The model is highly configurable, and particularly suitable for system-on-a-chip (SOC) designs. The full source code is available under the [GNU LGPL license](http://www.gnu.org/copyleft/lesser.html), allowing free and unlimited use in both research and commercial applications.*

LEON2 was developed by [Gaisler Research](), Sweden, for the [European Space Agency](http://www.esa.int/).
