From [The New Dictionary of Cultural Literacy](http://www.bartleby.com/59/3/nomanisanisl.html),

<em>  
<strong>No man is an island</strong></em>

<em> </em>

<em>No one is self-sufficient; everyone relies on others. This saying comes from a sermon by the seventeenth-century English author John Donne.</em>

A full version of the verse is:

<em>“No man is an island, entire of itself; every man is a piece of the continent, a part of the main. If a clod be washed away by the sea, Europe is the less, as well as if a promontory were, as well as if a manor of thy friend’s or of thine own were. Any man’s death diminishes me because I am involved in mankind; and therefore never send to know for whom the bell tolls; it tolls for thee…”</em>

[—](http://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/John_Donne) [John Donne](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Donne), from [Meditation XVII](http://www.online-literature.com/donne/409/) (1624)

But, well, I actually know this verse from <em>[About A Boy](http://www.about-a-boy.com/)</em> \[[IMDb](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0276751/)\].

Although I sometimes have a feeling that I am, an island. But I know that I’m not. Nobody does.
