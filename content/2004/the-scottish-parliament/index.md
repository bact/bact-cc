Tomorrow, October 9, 2004, there’ll be an opening ceremony of the new **Scottish Parliament** buildings at [Holyrood](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holyrood). See parliament homepage in [Scots](http://www.scottish.parliament.uk/vli/language/scots/).

At the same time, there’ll be [a demonstration](http://srsm.port5.com/news/october9.html), run by the [Scottish Republican Socialist Movement](http://srsm.port5.com/srsm/), for [Scottish independence](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_independence) at [Carlton Hill](http://www.edinburghguide.com/travel/visitor/caltonhill.htm).

Of related topic, here is the Lord Fraser’s report on the construction of the new parliament buildings — the [Holyrood Inquiry](http://www.holyroodinquiry.org/).
