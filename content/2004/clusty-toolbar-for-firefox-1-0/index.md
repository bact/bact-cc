“Group, not list.”

A [Firefox](http://GetFirefox.com/) plug-in from [Vivisimo](http://vivisimo.com/).

Besides search and cluster functionalities, it also bring you a definition of a word in the web page (from [Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/) or dictionary).

Btw, [Mozilla Firefox 1.0](http://www.mozilla.org/products/firefox/) is out now.
