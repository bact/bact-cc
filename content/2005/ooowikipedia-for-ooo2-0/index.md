Hey! This one is cool 😉

*[OOoWikipedia](http://www.indesko.com/sites/en/downloads/ooowikipedia/view) allows you to use [this famous encyclopedia](http://www.wikipedia.org/) directly from [OpenOffice.org](http://www.openoffice.org/). Simply highlight a word in a text portion, launch the tool and your browser will open the corresponding article in the encyclopedia, if the definition is available.*
