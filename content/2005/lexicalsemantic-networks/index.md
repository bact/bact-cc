**Sematic Networks**

- [Mimida](http://www.gittens.nl/SemanticNetworks.html) <span class="small">(many languages, including non-European ones)</span>
- [EuroWordNet](http://www.illc.uva.nl/EuroWordNet/) <span class="small">(Dutch, Italian, Spanish, German, French, Czech, Estonian)</span>
- [MultiWordNet](http://multiwordnet.itc.it/) <span class="small">(Italian, English, Spanish, Hebrew)</span>
- [WordNet](http://wordnet.princeton.edu/) <span class="small">(English)</span>
- [TCL’s Computational Lexicon](http://www.tcllab.org/Pages/tcllex.html) <span class="small">(Thai)</span>
- [GermaNet](http://www.sfs.nphil.uni-tuebingen.de/lsd/english.html) <span class="small">(German)</span>
- [WordNet for Hebrew](http://cl.haifa.ac.il/projects/mwn/index.shtml)
- [WordNet Domains](http://wndomains.itc.it/) each synset has been annotated with at least one domain label
- [Suggested Upper Merged Ontology (SUMO)](http://www.ontologyportal.org/)
- [Global WordNet Association](http://www.globalwordnet.org/)
