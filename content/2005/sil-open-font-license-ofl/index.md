[The SIL Open Font License (OFL)](http://scripts.sil.org/OFL) is a new free and open source license specifically designed for fonts and related software based on [SIL](http://sil.org/) experience in [font design](http://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&cat_id=TypeDesign) and linguistic software engineering.
