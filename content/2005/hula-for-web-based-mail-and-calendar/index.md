“[Hula](http://www.hula-project.org/) is a calendar and mail server. We are focused on building a calendar and mail server that people love to use, instead of broadly trying to build a “groupware server” that managers want to deploy.”

Hula is available under the [LGPL](http://www.gnu.org/copyleft/lesser.html) and [MPL.](http://www.mozilla.org/MPL/)
