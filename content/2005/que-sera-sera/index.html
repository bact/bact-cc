Que Sera, Sera,
Whatever will be, will be
The future’s not ours, to see
Que Sera, Sera
What will be, will be.

Or, in other (pessimistic) words:

“If anything can go wrong, it will.”
(Murphy’s Law)

Flows.
And consoles to all the victims and their relatives.
