Halo. Now in Haus 24, 1.82. [Institut für Linguistik](http://www.ling.uni-potsdam.de/), [Uni Potsdam](http://www.uni-potsdam.de/).

I’m going to work in the [Project SUMMaR](http://www.ling.uni-potsdam.de/cl/cl/res/forsch_summar.html).

From the project info page – “SUMMaR is part of the [BMBF](http://www.bmbf.de/) project [PINK (‘Plattform fuer INtelligente Kollaborationsportale’)](http://www.xmlcityberlin.de/index.php/m/tixml/t/0/c/0/i/147.html), a consortium of companies and universities from Berlin-Brandenburg, funded in the framework [‘Innovative regionale Wachstumskerne’](http://www.unternehmen-region.de/de/151.php).”

Travel info, this morning: U9 (underground) from *Leopoldplatz* to *Zoologischer Garten* station, then RE1 (rail) for *Potsdam Hbf*, then Bus 606 for *Golm Hbf*, and walk into the campus.  
Note: *‘Hbf’*, short for [*Hauptbahnhof*](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hauptbahnhof) <span class="small">([English translation](http://216.239.39.104/translate_c?hl=en&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&langpair=de%7Cen&u=http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hauptbahnhof&prev=/language_tools))</span>, is something like “central station / main station”.
