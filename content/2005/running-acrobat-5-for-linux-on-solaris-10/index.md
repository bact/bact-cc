[ใช้ Acrobat Reader สำหรับลีนุกซ์ บน โซลาริส 10](http://blogs.sun.com/roller/page/aland/20041214#linux_compatibility_on_amd64) (มีภาพหน้าจอ)

Alan DuBoff was running the Linux version of Adobe Acrobat Reader in Solaris 10 on AMD64. With its [linux compatibility](http://www.sun.com/software/solaris/ds/linux_interop.jsp), it is possible to run Linux applications on Solaris 10 out-of-the-box (no recompilation required).
