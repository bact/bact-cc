> [The SIIT *Image and Vision Computing Laboratory*](http://ivc.siit.tu.ac.th/) performs research on problems related to [computer vision](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision) and [image processing](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing). Our specialties are [biomedical image processing](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_image_processing) and [mobile robot vision](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robot), but we’re interested in pretty much anything to do with [pixels](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixel) and [intelligence](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_%28trait%29), [artificial](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) or otherwise.
