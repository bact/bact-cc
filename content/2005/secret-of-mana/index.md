ชอบมากๆ

*Secret of Mana* (English version name of *Seiken Densetsu*) is an “Action RPG” game on *Super Famicom* (or, outside Asia, *Super NES*) by [Square](http://www.square-enix.com/).

[Seed of Mana](http://www.planetnintendo.com/secretofmana/) is a fantastic fansite of the game. Contains artworks and musics from the game, history, news, and links. As well as a SNES emulator + ROMs. Another nice fansite is [Mana Knight’s](http://mmxz.zophar.net/rpg/mana/som.html).

GameFAQs has also more info about [Secret of Mana](http://www.gamefaqs.com/console/snes/data/588646.html) (Seiken Densetsu 2) and [Secret of Mana 2](http://www.gamefaqs.com/console/snes/data/588648.html) (Seiken Densetsu 3).

Note: The English name of the first Seiken Densetsu is *Final Fantasy Adventure*, available for GameBoy. *Legend of Mana* is Seiken Densetsu 4, PlayStation.

For Mana’s fanlisting, listen to [Destiny Calls](http://mana.1121.org/).

More Mana’s websites at [Seiken Densetsu Series](http://dmoz.org/Games/Video_Games/Roleplaying/S/Seiken_Densetsu_Series/) in dmoz.org.

It is my favorite action RPG of all time.
