[DOAJ](http://www.doaj.org/): Directory of Open Access Journals  
Free, full text, quality controlled scientific and scholarly journals service.  
Currently 1530 journals in the directory.  
Including Life Sciences, Business, Chemistry, Mathematics, Social  
Sciences, and Engineering.

[PLoS](http://plos.org/): Public Library of Science  
Publishes journals using Open Access License.  
One of them is *PLoS Computational Biology*.
