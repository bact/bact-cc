[An Interview with Ourmedia.org’s J.D. Lasica](http://www.oreillynet.com/pub/a/network/2005/07/15/ourmedia.html). Ourmedia.org is a [glassroots media](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grassroots_democracy) hosting provider, offers free storage and free bandwidth for videos, audio files, photos, text or software. Most of the media published is in some form of free/open licenses such as GPL, Public Domain, or Creative Commons.

The interview also mentioned [Flickr](http://www.oreillynet.com/pub/a/network/2005/02/04/sb_flckr.html).
