Doing human rights right at the internet infrastructure level. Wow.

Read about this for quite sometime, the works by IETF and friends, but mostly from ones that related to [HTTP/2 development](https://http2.github.io/faq/#what-does-http2-do-to-improve-security). Today I’m learning more on these efforts (and beyond) during APC Member Meeting 2017. Thanks to [Avri Doria](https://icannwiki.org/Avri_Doria).

- [RFC 6973 – Privacy Considerations for Internet Protocols](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc6973/)
- [RFC 7624 – Confidentiality in the Face of Pervasive Surveillance: A Threat Model and Problem Statement](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc7624/)
- [RFC 7626 – DNS Privacy Considerations](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc7626/)
- [RFC 7696 (BCP 201) – Guidelines for Cryptographic Algorithm Agility and Selecting Mandatory-to-Implement Algorithms](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc7696/)
- [RFC 7721 – Security and Privacy Considerations for IPv6 Address Generation Mechanisms](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc7721/)
- [RFC 7819 – Privacy Considerations for DHCP](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc7819/)
- [RFC 8065 – Privacy Considerations for IPv6 Adaptation-Layer Mechanisms](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc8065/)

Find out more RFC (request for comment) documents at [Internet Architecture Board – Privacy and Security Program](https://www.iab.org/activities/programs/privacy-and-security-program/).

More explanations on human rights and internet engineering standards here:

- **[The Privacy is in the Protocol: Why Civil Society Needs to Pay Attention to the IETF](https://www.freedomonlinecoalition.com/how-we-work/working-groups/working-group-1/wg-1-blog-4-ietf/)** by Mallory Knodel
- **[Engineering and lawyering privacy by design: understanding online privacy both as a technical and an international human rights issue](https://academic.oup.com/ijlit/article/24/4/374/2566975/Engineering-and-lawyering-privacy-by-design)** by Adamantia Rachovitsa.
