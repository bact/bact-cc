Sick. Sick. Sick.

[Arsenal](http://arsenal.com/ "the coolest club in the British isles") [got Rosicky](http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport1/hi/football/teams/a/arsenal/5008544.stm). Cool.

I got Low-Sicky. Uncool.

Low is mental. Sick is physical.

Frankly, Edinburgh’s weather is not that nice, I think we have only 2 months of real sunshine — but I never have a real long sick like this.  
Berlin’s weather seems to be better, at least when it’s hot, it’s a real hot, almost like in Thailand. But I don’t know why my health is very prone to it. Yesterday my nasal mucus is so green, first time in almost three years (the last time I can remember was my first winter – first snow experience – in Edinburgh).

Global warming ? Or just a lack of vitamin C ?  
(if it’s the later one, then stop buying [Hohes C](http://www.hohes-c.com/), since that means it doesn’t help anything.)

Another possibility is .. malnutrition ? … but, hey, I eat lots of vegetables na. All döner kebabs have lots of vegetables!

May be I just can’t live without 7-Eleven / 24-hours cheap (and good) food stalls.
