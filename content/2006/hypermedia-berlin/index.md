> **“Hypermedia Berlin”** is an interdisciplinary, multimedia, web-based research and curriculum development project, which investigates, analyzes, and maps the cultural, architectural, and historical layers of a city space.  
> The project takes <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin" data-reg="tag">Berlin</a> – arguably one of the most rich, contradictory, and multi-layered cities in the world – as its point of departure and seeks to develop an innovative research and pedagogical platform for studying and teaching Berlin’s history.

- [Hypermedia Berlin project](http://www.berlin.ucla.edu/)
- [Berlin: Temporal Topographies](http://shl.stanford.edu/research/berlin.html)
