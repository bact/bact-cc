WOS4 Workshop – Wiki 2.0: The Most Powerful Extensions for MediaWiki
14 Sep 2006, Erik Möller, on Wikidata, Semantic MediaWiki, LiquidThreads and more

part of Wizards of OS 4 : Information Freedom Rules
International conference, 14-16 Sep 2006 @ Columbiahalle, Berlin

  • MediaWiki 2.0 proposals
  • LiquidThreads — a better place to talk about wiki page
  • Wikiflow — a wiki-workflow mechanism, information goes thru different states (“published”, “stable”, “edit copy/live copy”, “deleted”, etc.)
  • Wikidata — one Wikidata to hold them all, a wiki-like database for various types of content
  • Ultimate Wiktionary data design — proposal for Wikidata-based Wiktionary
  • WiktionaryZ — a working prototype of next generation of Wiktionary, Wikidata “dog food”
  • WiktionaryZ blog + Ultimate Wiktionary blog — on development, system side and linguistics side
  • Semantic Wikipedia proposal / Uni Karlsruhe’s paper — ontology of wikipedia articles / typed links
