[WOS4 Workshop](http://wizards-of-os.org/index.php?id=2800&L=3 "Wizards of OS 4 Workshop") – [Wiki 2.0: The Most Powerful Extensions for MediaWiki](http://wizards-of-os.org/index.php?id=2823&L=3)  
14 Sep 2006, [Erik Möller](http://freedomdefined.org/User:Erik_Möller), on [Wikidata](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikidata), [Semantic MediaWiki](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Semantic_MediaWiki), [LiquidThreads](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/LiquidThreads) and more

part of [Wizards of OS 4](http://wizards-of-os.org/) : *Information Freedom Rules*  
International conference, 14-16 Sep 2006 @ Columbiahalle, Berlin

- [MediaWiki 2.0 proposals](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/MediaWiki_2.0)
- [LiquidThreads](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/LiquidThreads) — a better place to talk about wiki page
- [Wikiflow](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikiflow) — a wiki-workflow mechanism, information goes thru different states (“published”, “stable”, “edit copy/live copy”, “deleted”, etc.)
- [Wikidata](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikidata) — one Wikidata to hold them all, a wiki-like database for various types of content
- [Ultimate Wiktionary data design](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Ultimate_Wiktionary_data_design) — proposal for Wikidata-based Wiktionary
- [WiktionaryZ](http://wiktionaryz.org/) — a working prototype of next generation of Wiktionary, Wikidata “dog food”
- [WiktionaryZ blog](http://wiktionaryz.blogspot.com/) + [Ultimate Wiktionary blog](http://ultimategerardm.blogspot.com/) — on development, system side and linguistics side
- [Semantic Wikipedia proposal](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Semantic_Wikipedia) / [Uni Karlsruhe’s paper](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/Publikationen/showPublikation_english?publ_id=1055) — ontology of wikipedia articles / typed links
