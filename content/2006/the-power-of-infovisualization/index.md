A great presentation that shows the power of [information visualization](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_visualization) and how computer technology and good design could deliver a better understanding of the world situation to its dwellers:

[Ideas Worth Spreading: Hans Rosling from TED 2006](http://video.google.com/videoplay?docid=2670820702819322251 "@ Google Video")
