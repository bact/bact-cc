[Kosmix](http://www.kosmix.com/) is a topic-specific search engine, that gives you results in a particular viewpoint of your choice.

Search for “[global warming](http://www.kosmix.com/search?q=global+warming&t=vpolitics.any&out=politics&lid=index)” in [Politics](http://www.kosmix.com/index_politics.html), it will give you views of Libertarian, Liberal, and Conservative.

The same goes for other two topics, [Health](http://www.kosmix.com/index_health.html) (basic info, alternative medicine, diet & nutrition, etc.) and [Travel](http://www.kosmix.com/index_travel.html) (hotels, travels guides, museums, etc.).
