มกราคม 2549 เบอร์ลินได้รับเลือกจากยูเนสโกให้เป็น “เมืองแห่งการออกแบบ”
January 2006, UNESCO awarded Berlin the title of “City of Design”

hubs / zines

  • DESIGNMAI — Berlin’s international design festival, held every May (Mai)
  • berlindesign.net — the network of Berlin product designers
  • berlinomat — platform für Berlinder design
  • BERLIN — the magazine from the capital. The theme of issue no. 2 (May 2005) “Berlin Design”
  • Internationale Design Zentrum Berlin — events, exhibition, management, awards

blogs

  • Dreaming of a City: A Letter from Berlin — a rather long blog at Core77 about DESIGNMAI 2006
  • Ding-Pong – a Zeit.de (German newspaper) design blog, lots of Berlin-related design posts

museums / galleries

  • Bauhaus-Archive Museum für Gestaltung — Museum of Design, exhibits from the world famous design school
  • Staatliche Museen zu Berlin — Berlin state museums and galleries
  • Neue Nationalgalerie — New National Gallery, early modern art to art of the 1960s + special temporary exhibitions
  • Hamburger Bahnhof – Museum für Gegenwart — “Museum for the Present”, contemporary art, second half of the 20th century onwards + special temporary exhibitions
  • Akademie der Künste — Academy of the Arts, society + collections + library + etc. on 6 sections: fine arts, architecture, music, literature, visual arts, and film
