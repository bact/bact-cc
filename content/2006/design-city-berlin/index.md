มกราคม 2549 เบอร์ลินได้รับเลือกจากยูเนสโกให้เป็น “เมืองแห่งการออกแบบ”  
January 2006, [UNESCO awarded Berlin the title of “City of Design”](http://portal.unesco.org/culture/en/ev.php-URL_ID=29376&URL_DO=DO_TOPIC&URL_SECTION=201.html)

**hubs / zines**

- [DESIGNMAI](http://www.designmai.de/) — Berlin’s international design festival, held every May (Mai)
- [berlindesign.net](http://www.berlindesign.net/) — the network of Berlin product designers
- [berlinomat](http://www.berlinomat.com/) — platform für Berlinder design
- [BERLIN](http://www.berlin-magazin.net/) — the magazine from the capital. The theme of issue no. 2 (May 2005) “Berlin Design”
- [Internationale Design Zentrum Berlin](http://www.idz.de/) — events, exhibition, management, awards

**blogs**

- [Dreaming of a City: A Letter from Berlin](http://www.core77.com/reactor/06.06_designmai.asp) — a rather long blog at Core77 about DESIGNMAI 2006
- [Ding-Pong](http://blog.zeit.de/design/) – a Zeit.de <span class="small">(German newspaper)</span> design blog, lots of Berlin-related design posts

**museums / galleries**

- [Bauhaus-Archive Museum für Gestaltung](http://www.bauhaus.de/) — Museum of Design, exhibits from the world famous design school
- [Staatliche Museen zu Berlin](http://www.museen-berlin.de/) — Berlin state museums and galleries
- [Neue Nationalgalerie](http://www.neue-nationalgalerie.de/) — New National Gallery, early modern art to art of the 1960s + special temporary exhibitions
- [Hamburger Bahnhof – Museum für Gegenwart](http://www.hamburgerbahnhof.de/) — “Museum for the Present”, contemporary art, second half of the 20th century onwards + special temporary exhibitions
- [Akademie der Künste](http://www.adk.de/) — Academy of the Arts, society + collections + library + etc. on 6 sections: fine arts, architecture, music, literature, visual arts, and film
