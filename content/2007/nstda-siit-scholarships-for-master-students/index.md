More than 20 [NSTDA](http://www.nstda.or.th/ "National Science and Technology Development Agency")–[SIIT](http://www.siit.tu.ac.th/ "Sirindhorn International Institute of Technology, Thammasat University") scholarships, covering tuitions and educational support fees, are available for SIIT master programs.

SIIT offers degrees in Civil Engineering, Environmental Technology, Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Engineering Management, Management Technology, Information Technology, Computer Science, and Communications.

More info, contact the academic programs or [faculty members](http://www.siit.tu.ac.th/dirctory/full_fac.html) in the [fields of your interest](http://www.siit.tu.ac.th/publicat/graduate.pdf).
