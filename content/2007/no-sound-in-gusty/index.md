Gusty Gibbon pre-releases have at least one serious audio problem — [they make no sound at all](https://bugs.launchpad.net/ubuntu/+source/gstreamer/+bug/131711).

Look at [“Bugs assigned Ubuntu Audio Team” list](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-audio/+assignedbugs), you can find lots of bugs titled with “no sound”, “sound lost”, “sound missing”, … and Google results of “[no sound ubuntu gusty](http://www.google.co.th/search?q=no+sound+ubuntu+gusty)” do pretty the same.

My VAIO PCG-V505CP faced this problem as well in recent weeks (after upgrading from Ubuntu 7.06), but after make a fresh install with 7.10 Tribe 5 Live CD, it works again.

Happy working with music.
