April 2, 2007

Director’s Position Search for the Sirindhorn International Institute of Technology, Thammsat University

Sirindhorn International Institute of Technology, Thammasat University seeks applications for the position of Director of the Institute. The Director is responsible for providing academic and scholarly leadership and administration to the Institute. The Director reports to the Board of Trustees of the Institute which is under the Thammasat University Council. Candidates for this position should possess the following qualifications :

  • Hold Thai nationality.
  • Hold the academic rank of Professor in a field related to the program(s) offered at the Institute.
  • Have ability to raise funds.
  • Have academic management ability.
  • Have ability to initiate cooperation with other institutions for the benefit of SIIT.

Applications will be accepted until June 4, 2007. Review of applications will begin after June 4, 2007 and continue until an appointment is made. Each applicant shall submit an application letter together with his/her resume and vision statement. The materials should be sent by post to:

Prof. Dr. Somnuk Tangtermsirikul
Secretary of the SIIT Director Searching Committee
Sirindhorn International Institute of Technology
Thammasat University, Rangsit Campus
Klong Luang, Patumthani 12120
e-mail : somnuk AT siit tu ac th
www.siit.tu.ac.th

For more details please contact Prof. Dr. Somnuk Tangtermsirikul at 02-9869009 ext 1902.
